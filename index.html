01

You describe it

One sentence. What comes back is a running world — not a slideshow of frames.

02

A live world appears

The preview is the game. You can play it while you talk. That is the whole trick.

03

You steer without killing the run

Chat or code. Edits show up in the world that’s already going. No restart. No lost place.

04

A crew builds with you

Feel, look, play. They do the unglamorous passes. You stay the director.

05

You ship a link

Publish to play.ontrike.com. Strangers play first. You keep the git.

Same product, four doors
