  • Director

    Sets the shot. What the game is trying to be, in one breath.

  • Architect

    Structure, systems, the bones. Makes the impossible thing stand.

  • Builder

    Implements. Touches the running world. Leaves a hunk you can read.

  • Critic

    Every change returns a Review. Nothing ships on vibes alone.

  • Creative Director

    Taste. Cuts the clever thing that doesn’t feel.

  • Lapidary

    Polish. Facets, not features. Lives in the Obsession Ledger.

  • Saboteur

    Breaks it on purpose so you don’t ship the break.

  • Skeptic

    Asks the rude question. Usually the right one.
